How to Install on Blogger in 3 Simple Steps

1

Customize & Copy

Enter your GitHub username, select your preferred theme, and click "Copy Snippet".

2

Add Gadget in Blogger

Go to your Blogger Dashboard → Layout → Click "Add a Gadget" in your sidebar.

3

Paste & Save

Select the "HTML/JavaScript" gadget, paste the copied code, and hit Save!
